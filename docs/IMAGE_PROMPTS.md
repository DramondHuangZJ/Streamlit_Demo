# Product image generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, not a CLI/API fallback. Generated outputs were copied into this repository's `assets/` folder; they are not dependent on the original generation directory. Both selected outputs are 1536×1024 PNGs. Reference photographs are listed in MATERIALS.md and were inspected before generation.

## Dragon Castle

References: official component overview and official tabletop tile photograph.

> Use case: product-mockup. Create one realistic, faithful research product rendering of the real Dragon Castle board game shown in these official reference photos. References specify actual product appearance; do not redesign anything. Show recognizable actual Dragon Castle box at rear left, central multi-level castle built from ivory rectangular Mahjong-inspired resin tiles with the game's green, red, yellow, blue, black and purple symbols, one illustrated realm board with a small built group and dark green shrine pieces, spare tiles and tokens. Preserve distinctive actual board/box appearance and real component shapes from references. Do not substitute generic Mahjong or fabricate extra architecture or dragons standing on the table. Composition: plain light neutral tabletop, clean off-white background, approximately 35-degree slightly elevated three-quarter catalog camera, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, entire displayed product set in frame with generous equal margin, soft evenly diffused daylight, factual non-promotional presentation. No people, no hands, no scenery, no decorative props, no awards, no slogans, no overlay captions, no watermarks. Texture should look like the real mass-produced board game, not luxurious upgraded materials. One product scene, not a collage. This will be paired with an Azul rendering using the same background, lighting and framing.

Selected asset: `assets/dragon_castle_product_v1.png`.

## Inspection and limitations

Both renderings were inspected against their official references for product identity, component shapes, major colors and composition. They remain labelled AI renderings. Fine markings, exact tile/board geometry and rendered component arrangements are not guaranteed authentic. Official rulebook figure captures supply the authoritative board/component details on learning pages. Researcher review against a physical copy or permitted official photograph is required before recruitment. No AI participant assistance is enabled.
